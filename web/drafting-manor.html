    Rules
    • Steps: entering a room costs 1. To reach a distant door you walk the shortest path automatically, paying 1 step per room. The run ends at 0.
    • Locked doors: cost 1 key to open. Whether a door is locked is rolled by rank when the door appears, and it is visible from the start.
    • Drafting: opening a door draws 3 different floorplans from the deck. A rarity is picked by weight first, then one of the remaining rooms of that rarity at random. The first slot is a free room when possible, and costs nothing even when it is not. The three are never all dead ends. A draft cannot be cancelled.
    • Placement: rooms whose doors would face outside the house are never offered. L-shaped rooms always turn their fixed way (left or right); T-shaped rooms get a random valid orientation.
    • Connecting: if a new room's door meets an existing door, the two join and open, even if locked. If it meets a wall, it is blocked. The Antechamber can only be reached this way, and walking into it costs 3 keys.
    • Resources: gained once, when the room is placed. Some red rooms cost steps.
    • AI reward: 2r−1/256 the first time you reach rank r, +10 for reaching the goal. This previews the reward planned for the RL environment.

    Rarity weights

    The deck holds each of the 99 rooms exactly once. A placed room leaves the deck for the rest of the run.

    How to play

    You build a manor one room at a time, choosing which door to open and which room to put behind it.

    Goal: reach the Antechamber at the top of the plan. Build rooms until one of them connects to an Antechamber door, then walk in by spending 3 keys.

    1. Pick a door.

      Click a door on the edge of your rooms. White is open; yellow is locked and costs 1 key.

    2. Open it.

      An Open button appears in the dashed empty cell behind the door. Walking there costs 1 step per room you pass through, plus 1 step to enter the new room.

    3. Draft one of three rooms.

      Each card shows the room's doors (the yellow triangle is where you walk in), its Cost and its Reward. You must pick one; drafts cannot be cancelled.

    4. Keep going up.

      New doors are more likely to be locked the higher you go (the % on the right of the plan). Plan a route, not just the next room.
